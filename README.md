# 👻 Ghost Mod V5: Every Mind — a Minecraft Bedrock add-on

When you die, you come back as a **see-through black-and-white ghost with bright white glowing eyes**. Ghosts haunt the living and take over mobs:

- 🧟 **Corpses** (mobs that just died) can be possessed **instantly**. Tap the corpse and you're inside it.
- 🧠 **Living mobs** have to be conquered from the inside. **Dive into the mob's brain**, which pulls you into **the Mind Realm**, the add-on's **own dimension**, and beat its **mind trial**. Win, and you wake up inside its body.

It works on **phones and tablets (Android and iOS)**, Windows, Xbox, PlayStation and Switch. Everything is **tap to use**, so you don't need a keyboard.

> **New in V5: Every Mind:**
> - **Possess almost every mob in the game: 87 of them.** Horses, cats, axolotls, pandas, fish, the warden, the Wither, and more. The full list is below.
> - **Inside a mind you can jump 2 blocks high**, and every raised ledge in the Memory Hunt has proper steps, so no trial is impossible any more.
> - **Fixed:** the blaze and ghast powers now work. Minecraft doesn't let add-ons spawn fireballs, so they now fire as instant fire bolts and explosions where you aim.
> - Lots of new powers: Gallop, Spit, Ink Cloud, Shell Up, Rage, Sonic Boom, Wither Skull, Guardian Beam, Levitation Bolt and more.
>
> **V4** moved minds into **the Mind Realm**, a dimension of their own. They're no longer in The End, so **no more endermen** wander in. Nothing spawns in the Mind Realm except what the trial puts there.
>
> **V3.1** added:
> - 🎬 **Cinematic possession animations.**
> - 🧠 **Every mind looks like its mob.** You're inside a **giant copy of the mob's own head**, built block by block from its real face.
>
> **V3** added the Mind Dive with four mind trials and removed Essence.

## 📱 Install on your phone

1. Download **[`dist/GhostModV5.mcaddon`](dist/GhostModV5.mcaddon)** (tap the file on GitHub, then tap **Download raw file**).
2. Open it:
   - **Android:** tap the file and choose **Minecraft**.
   - **iPhone/iPad:** open the **Files** app, tap the file, then tap **Share → Minecraft**.
3. Edit your world. Under **Behavior Packs**, activate **Ghost Mod V5: Every Mind (Behavior)**. The resource pack is turned on automatically.
4. **V5 updates V3 and V4 in place**, so ghosts, graves and corpses carry over. If the world still lists an older Ghost Mod, remove it and add V5. Only one version should be active at a time.
5. Play. You **don't** need any experimental toggles.

You need **Minecraft 1.26.30 or newer** (any version from June 2026 on). That's the first version that lets add-ons create their own dimension.

## 👻 Being a ghost

- A **gravestone** marks where you died, and you rise above it as a black-and-white wraith with **glowing white eyes**.
- You can't be hurt, and you can't break blocks or use normal items.
- **Floating:** hold **Jump** to rise. You glide down slowly. **Sneak** to drop fast.

| Slot | Item | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Spirit Reaper** | Hit a living player to drain their life. In a Spirit Duel, it's your weapon |
| 2 | **Haunting Hand** | Tap blocks to haunt them: doors slam, levers flip, candles flicker, bells toll, TNT hisses. Tap the air for **Spirit Sense**: points to players, corpses and minds you can invade. Hit a mob to give it an invisible shove |
| 3 | **🧠 Mind Dive** | **Hit a living mob, or look at it and tap, to dive into its brain** |
| 4 | **Wailing Bell** | A terrifying wail that blinds and scares everyone nearby |
| 5 | **Spectral Wisp** | Dash where you look (look up to fly up) |
| 6 | **Phase Veil** | Walk through walls for 5 seconds |
| 9 | **Soul Heart** | **Revive** once it recharges (2 minutes). Your HUD shows the countdown |

A living friend can also revive you at any time by tapping your gravestone while holding a **Golden Apple**.

## 🎬 Possession animations

- **Mind Breach** (diving into a living mob):
  - The camera leaves your body and swings around the entranced mob as soul spirals coil around it and it shivers.
  - Then it rushes **into the mob's eyes** with a warden-style sonic boom and a pink flash, and you fall into its mind.
- **Establishing shot:** inside the mind, the camera starts high up, facing the **giant face of the mob**, then swoops down into your eyes.
- **Soul Plunge** (tapping a corpse): the camera rises above the corpse while your soul streams into it. The corpse **stands up, its eyes ignite white**, and the camera dives into its head.
- **Awakening** (winning a mind trial): you watch the conquered body rise with your glowing eyes before slipping inside.

You can't move during the animations (2 to 3 seconds). Control comes back automatically.

## 🧠 Mind Dive: getting through a mob's brain

1. Hit a living mob with the **Mind Dive**.
2. The mob freezes in a trance (`Entranced Zombie`), and the **Mind Breach** animation pulls you through a dimension loading screen into **the Mind Realm**. This empty void dimension belongs to the add-on, and nothing spawns there.
3. Its mind is **a giant hollow copy of its own head**, 32 blocks across. Every wall, the floor and the ceiling are its real face and skin, turned into pixel art made of the closest-coloured blocks:
   - **Zombie:** a huge green face with dark eyes.
   - **Creeper:** its famous face.
   - **Pig:** a pink room.
   - **Enderman:** pitch black with **glowing purple eyes**.
   - **Spider:** glowing red eyes.

   Bright eyes are built from glowing blocks, so they light up the mind. The trial's platforms and walls use the mob's own colours, and its sounds echo around the head.
4. Inside the mind you look like **your living self**, light as a thought: **you can jump 2 blocks high**.
4. **Beat the trial in time.** You wake up back in the overworld, **inside the mob's body**, and its eyes glow white.
5. **Fail** (or tap **Escape Mind**) and you're **expelled**. The mob wakes up and its mind stays **guarded** for 60 seconds.
6. If someone **kills the body** while you're in its mind, you return and its **corpse** is waiting for you to possess.

### The four mind trials

| Trial | Mostly for | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| ⚔️ **Spirit Duel** | Monsters and fighters: zombies, skeletons, spiders, the warden, the ravager, pillagers and more | Fight the mob's **empowered Spirit**, which waits in front of its own giant face, using your Spirit Reaper. Each hit it lands costs a bar of **Mind**, and losing all 6 gets you expelled. Neutral spirits (wolf, golem, bear) only fight back once you strike first |
| 🏃 **Mind Parkour** | Flyers and jumpers: blaze, ghast, phantom, Wither, parrot, bee, rabbit, goat, creeper | Climb a **spiral of floating thoughts** up the inside of its head to the glowing **Brain Core**. Don't touch the floor of its mind: you get 3 tries. There's a glowing checkpoint halfway |
| 🌀 **Thought Maze** | Clever and odd minds: enderman, villagers, piglins, horses, llamas, the creaking, and many animals | A randomly generated maze. Find the **Brain Core** before time runs out (your HUD shows which way to go) |
| ✨ **Memory Hunt** | Fish, squids and other sea creatures, plus farm animals like cows, sheep and chickens | Catch all **6 glowing memories**, some on raised ledges with steps up to them, before they fade |

Each mob's trial is listed in the [table of every possessable mob](#-possessing-a-body).

Every mind is generated fresh, so no two dives are the same.

## 🧟 Possessing a body

Almost every mob (87 kinds) leaves a **corpse** when it dies near a ghost. The corpse lies on the ground (`Corpse of Zombie`). **Tap it** to possess it instantly, with no mind trial needed.

Inside a body:

- **Walk** with the joystick, and **Jump** to jump. Flying mobs fly where you look.
- **Hit** things with the mob's real strength.
- Tap **Body Power** (slot 1) for the mob's special ability.
- Leave with **Leave Body** (slot 2) or the **dismount/sneak** button. The body becomes a corpse you can re-enter later.
- If the body dies, you pop back out as a ghost.

## ⚙️ Admin commands

These need cheats or operator permission.

| Command | Effect |
|---|---|
| `/scriptevent ghost:become [player]` | Turn yourself or a named player into a ghost |
| `/scriptevent ghost:revive [player]` | Revive a ghost instantly |
| `/scriptevent ghost:corpse <body>` | Drop a corpse at your feet (e.g. `zombie`, `blaze`) |
| `/scriptevent ghost:siphon off` / `on` | Turn the ghost life-drain on players off or on |
| `/scriptevent ghost:help` | Show the ghost guide in chat |

To change the settings, edit the `CFG`, `MIND` and `TRIALS` blocks at the top of `ghost_bp/scripts/main.js`: revive time, trial time limits, mind guard time, and whether explosions break blocks. Each body's power and mind trial is in the `BODIES` table.

## 🛠️ For developers

```
ghost_bp/                       behavior pack: script, items, entities (+ entities/vessels/ = possessable bodies)
ghost_rp/                       resource pack: shroud and glowing eyes, bodies, memory orbs, gravestone
tools/generate_vessels.py       builds all 87 possessable bodies from Mojang's official vanilla packs: 27 hand-tuned, the rest configured automatically (scripts/bodies_auto.js)
tools/generate_minds.py         turns each mob's real head texture into block pixel art (ghost_bp/scripts/mind_heads.js)
tools/generate_textures.py      regenerates the pixel-art textures (needs Pillow)
tools/build.py                  packages dist/GhostModV5.mcaddon and the separate .mcpack files
```

- **Mind Realm:** a custom dimension (`ghost:mind_realm`) registered in `system.beforeEvents.startup` with `dimensionRegistry.registerCustomDimension`. It's a stable API since `@minecraft/server` 2.8.0.
  - The dimension uses the void generator, and each dive gets its own arena slot.
  - An `entitySpawn` guard removes any mob that appears there except the trial's spirit, its summons and the memories. If a game can't create the dimension, minds fall back to The End.
  - The arena is built with `Dimension.fillBlocks` once its chunks have loaded.
  - The mind is a 32-block room whose six faces are the mob's head faces. `generate_minds.py` reads the head cube's UVs from the vanilla model, crops the face pixels from the vanilla texture, and maps them to the nearest block colour. Glowing blocks are used for bright eyes.
  - Camera animations use the `minecraft:free` camera preset with easing, `camera.fade`, `/camerashake`, and a movement input lock.
  - It's torn down when you leave.
  - While the player is away, the real mob is held in a trance: a name tag keeps it from despawning, and resistance and slowness keep it in place. Winning replaces it with a possessable body.
- **Ghost look:** Invisibility plus a locked **Ghost Shroud** attachable, with a second emissive **eye layer** (vanilla `breeze_eyes` material with `ignore_lighting`).
- Uses only the **stable** Script API (`@minecraft/server` 2.8.0), so no experiments are needed.

Rebuild after you make changes:

```bash
python3 tools/generate_vessels.py    # only if you changed the body list
python3 tools/generate_minds.py      # only if you changed the body list
python3 tools/generate_textures.py   # only if you changed textures
python3 tools/build.py
```
