# 👻 Ghost Mod V2: Possession — a Minecraft Bedrock add-on

When you die, you come back as a **see-through, black-and-white ghost with bright white glowing eyes**. As a ghost you can hunt **Lost Souls** and **Haunted Blocks**, haunt and scare living players, and **climb inside the corpses of dead mobs** to use their bodies and powers. Once you have enough Soul Essence, you return to life.

It works on **phones and tablets (Android and iOS)**, Windows, Xbox, PlayStation and Switch. Every power is an item you just **tap**, so you don't need a keyboard.

> **New in V2:** possess 27 different mob corpses (each with its own power), glowing white eyes on ghosts and on possessed mobs, corpses that lie on the ground, a redesigned black-and-white wraith look, and darker lost souls.

---

## 📱 Install on your phone

1. Download **[`dist/GhostModV2.mcaddon`](dist/GhostModV2.mcaddon)** (tap the file on GitHub, then tap **Download raw file**).
2. Open it:
   - **Android:** tap the downloaded file and choose **Minecraft**.
   - **iPhone/iPad:** open the **Files** app, tap the file, then tap **Share → Minecraft**.
3. Minecraft imports **Ghost Mod V2: Possession (Behavior)** and **(Resources)**.
4. Edit your world. Under **Behavior Packs**, activate **Ghost Mod V2: Possession (Behavior)**. The resource pack is turned on automatically.
5. **If the first Ghost Mod is turned on in that world, remove it.** Only one version should be active at a time.
6. Play. You **don't** need any experimental toggles.

You need **Minecraft 1.26 or newer**, the current version. Phones update automatically. The possessed mobs reuse Minecraft's own mob models, so the add-on needs a current game version.

## 👻 What happens when you die

- A **gravestone** (`R.I.P. <name>`) appears where you died, and you rise above it as a **ghost**:
  - You look like a transparent black-and-white wraith: a black hood, a smoky robe and a misty white tail, with **bright white glowing eyes** that show even in the dark.
  - You can't be hurt, you don't get hungry, and you can see in the dark.
  - You can't break blocks or use normal items.
- **Floating:** hold **Jump** to rise. You glide down slowly. **Sneak** to drop fast.

## 🧟 Possession

When a mob dies near a ghost, it leaves a **corpse** lying on the ground, labelled `Corpse of Zombie` and so on. Corpses last 5 minutes.

1. **Tap the corpse**, or press the **Possess** button, to climb inside it. The corpse stands up, and **its eyes glow bright white**.
2. **Walk with the joystick.** The body goes where you go. It climbs 1-block steps by itself, and **Jump** jumps.
3. **Hit things** to attack with the mob's real strength.
4. Tap **Body Power** (slot 1) to use the mob's special ability.
5. **Leave** with **Leave Body** (slot 2) or the **dismount/sneak** button. The body falls back down as a corpse that you can re-enter later.
6. The body has the mob's own health. **If it dies, you pop back out** as a ghost.
7. You can't be seen inside the body, so other players only see a zombie or a cow with glowing eyes. **Killing a living player while possessing earns +3 Essence.**

## 🎯 Hunting & haunting

- **Lost Souls:** every mob that dies near a ghost releases a soul. Hunt it with the Spirit Reaper for Essence: 1 for normal mobs, 2 for monsters, 10 for bosses, and **double at night**.
- **Haunted Blocks:** blue soul flames mark them, and your HUD shows the way. Tapping one with the Haunting Hand gives **+3 Essence**. Some are **cursed** and release extra souls.
- **Haunting:** tap blocks with the Haunting Hand to make doors creak and slam, levers flip, candles and campfires flicker, note blocks and bells ring, chests rattle and TNT hiss.
- Living players feel a **cold presence** when a ghost is near.

## ❤️ Coming back to life

- **Soul Heart:** spend 10 Essence, or wait 10 minutes and revive for free.
- **Friend revive:** a living friend taps your gravestone while holding a **Golden Apple**.

## ⚙️ Admin commands

These need cheats or operator permission.

| Command | Effect |
|---|---|
| `/scriptevent ghost:become [player]` | Turn yourself or a named player into a ghost |
| `/scriptevent ghost:revive [player]` | Revive a ghost instantly |
| `/scriptevent ghost:essence <n>` | Give yourself Essence |
| `/scriptevent ghost:corpse <body>` | Drop a corpse at your feet, e.g. `zombie`, `blaze`, `creeper`. Leave the name out to list all bodies |
| `/scriptevent ghost:siphon off` / `on` | Turn the ghost life-siphon on players off or on |
| `/scriptevent ghost:help` | Show the ghost guide in chat |

To change the balance, edit the `CFG` block at the top of `ghost_bp/scripts/main.js`: revive cost, timers, corpse lifetime, cooldowns, and whether explosions or TNT haunting break blocks. Each body's damage, power and cooldown is in the `BODIES` table.

## 🛠️ For developers

```
ghost_bp/                       behavior pack: script, items, entities (+ entities/vessels/ = possessable bodies)
ghost_rp/                       resource pack: shroud and eye layers, lost soul, gravestone, possessable bodies
tools/generate_vessels.py       builds the 27 possessable bodies from Mojang's official vanilla packs
tools/generate_textures.py      regenerates the pixel-art textures (needs Pillow)
tools/build.py                  packages dist/GhostModV2.mcaddon and the separate .mcpack files
```

- **Ghost look:** the ghost has Invisibility and wears a locked **Ghost Shroud** in the chest slot. Its attachable draws a translucent wraith body, plus a second layer with **bright white eyes**. The eye layer uses vanilla's `breeze_eyes` material with `ignore_lighting`, so it glows at night.
- **Possessable bodies:** each body is a custom entity that copies a vanilla mob's look (model, animations, sounds) but has no AI.
  - The `ghost:corpse` property switches it between a **corpse** and a **possessed** body.
  - A corpse lies down using a rotation animation on an added `ghost_root` bone.
  - A possessed body can be ridden and steered with `input_ground_controlled`. Flying bodies use `free_camera_controlled` plus script steering from the player's joystick input.
  - Possessed bodies get an extra glowing-eye layer.
- Uses only the **stable** Script API (`@minecraft/server` 1.19.0), so no experiments are needed.

Rebuild after you make changes:

```bash
python3 tools/generate_vessels.py    # only if you changed the body list (downloads Mojang's samples once)
python3 tools/generate_textures.py   # only if you changed textures
python3 tools/build.py
```
